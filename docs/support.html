About OhMyCatch

OhMyCatch is a local-first personal fishing journal for iPhone. Your catches, photos, and spots stay on your device. No OhMyCatch account is required.

AI Fish ID & Premium

AI Fish ID is an optional Premium feature that analyzes your catch photos to suggest likely species matches. It requires an active internet connection, a selected catch photo, and an active Premium subscription (including 250 recognitions per monthly quota period). AI suggestions are informational; always confirm or correct the species before saving.

Restore Purchases

If you completed a subscription purchase but Premium features appear locked, open the subscription sheet in the app and tap Restore purchases. Also ensure your device is signed into the Apple Account used for the purchase.

Managing Subscriptions

Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your iPhone’s Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions, or via the in-app “Manage subscription” button in Settings.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, inside the OhMyCatch app sandbox (local database and files). Uninstalling the app removes that local data.

Why does OhMyCatch ask for location, camera, or photos?

  • Location: optional, to show you on the map and record catch locations you choose
  • Camera / Photos: optional, to attach catch photos or save catch cards to your library

You can deny these permissions and still use the journal with reduced features.

Platform requirements

  • iPhone (iOS 17.0 or later)
  • Portrait orientation
  • Free download with optional auto-renewable subscriptions (OhMyCatch Premium)

关于 OhMyCatch

OhMyCatch 是一款面向 iPhone 的本地优先个人钓鱼日记。你的渔获、照片和钓点均保存在本地设备上，无需注册 OhMyCatch 账户。

AI 鱼种识别与高级版订阅

AI 鱼种识别是一项可选的高级版（Premium）功能，可分析鱼获照片并给出可能的鱼种建议。使用该功能需要连接网络、选择鱼获照片以及持有有效的高级版订阅（每个月度额度周期包含最多 250 次识别）。AI 建议仅供参考，保存前请自行确认或修改鱼种。

恢复购买

如果你已完成付款但高级版功能仍未解锁，请在应用内打开订阅页面，点击“恢复购买”。同时请确认当前设备登录的是完成购买时的 Apple 账户。

管理与取消订阅

除非在当前计费周期结束前至少 24 小时取消，否则订阅将自动续订。你可以随时在 iPhone 的“设置” > [你的姓名/Apple 账户] > “订阅”中管理或取消自动续订，也可通过应用内“设置”中的“管理订阅”入口跳转。

常见问题

我的数据保存在哪里？

在你的 iPhone 上，位于 OhMyCatch 应用沙盒内（本地数据库和文件）。卸载应用会清除这些本地数据。

为什么 OhMyCatch 会请求位置、相机或相册权限？

  • 位置：可选，用于在地图上显示你的位置并记录你选择的渔获地点
  • 相机 / 相册：可选，用于附加渔获照片或把渔获卡片保存到相册

你可以拒绝这些权限，仍可使用基础日记功能。

平台要求

  • iPhone（iOS 17.0 或更高版本）
  • 竖屏
  • 免费下载，提供可选的自动续期订阅（OhMyCatch 高级版）
