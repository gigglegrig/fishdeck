FishDeck Support

Applies to: FishDeck iOS v1.0 (`com.gigglegrig.fishdeck`)

Last updated: 2026-07-15

About FishDeck

FishDeck is a local-first personal fishing journal for iPhone. Your catches, photos, and spots stay on your device in v1.0. There is no FishDeck account and no FishDeck cloud sync in this release.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, inside the FishDeck app sandbox (local database and files). Uninstalling the app removes that local data.

Why does FishDeck ask for location, camera, or photos?

  • **Location:** optional, to show you on the map and record catch locations you choose
  • **Camera / Photos:** optional, to attach catch photos or save catch cards to your library

You can deny these permissions and still use the journal with reduced features.

Can I recover data after deleting the app?

Not in v1.0. There is no FishDeck cloud backup. Keep your own exports or photos if you need a copy outside the app.

Does FishDeck track me or show ads?

No. v1.0 does not include FishDeck analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Platform requirements

  • iPhone (iPhone-only build)
  • Portrait orientation
  • Free download (no in-app purchases in v1.0)
