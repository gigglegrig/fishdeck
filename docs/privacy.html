FishDeck Privacy Policy

Applies to: FishDeck iOS v1.0 local-first release (`com.gigglegrig.fishdeck`)

Last updated: 2026-07-15

Effective date: 2026-07-16

This policy describes the shipped local-first v1.0 product. It does not cover future account, cloud sync, analytics, ads, tracking, AI, or social features.

Who we are

FishDeck is a personal fishing journal app published by gigglegrig (“we”, “us”).

Contact for privacy questions: open an issue at https://github.com/gigglegrig/fishdeck/issues (include “Privacy” in the title).

Summary

FishDeck v1.0 stores your fishing catch records on your device. We do not require an account. We do not operate a FishDeck cloud backend for catch data in v1.0. We do not sell your data. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in v1.0.

Information stored on your device

Depending on how you use the app, FishDeck may store on your device:

  • Catch records you create (species, date/time, notes, measurements, and related journal fields)
  • Photos you attach to catches (camera or photo library)
  • Location coordinates you choose to record for a catch, or that are read from a photo’s EXIF metadata when available
  • Local preferences and UI session state (for example filters or last-used views)
  • Bundled catalog and artwork that ships with the app

This information stays in on-device storage (including local SQLite and app documents). Uninstalling FishDeck removes the app’s local data from that device, subject to normal iOS storage behavior.

Permissions you control

FishDeck may ask for:

PermissionWhy
Location (When In Use)Show your position on the map and record catch locations when you choose
CameraTake catch photos
Photo LibraryAttach existing photos to catches
Photo Library AddSave catch cards or photos to your library when you choose

You can deny any permission. Core journaling still works without location or photos; related features are limited until you allow them.

Network use

FishDeck v1.0 does not upload your catch journal, photos, notes, or exact fishing coordinates to a FishDeck server.

The app may use Apple MapKit / Apple Maps tile and related map services through `react-native-maps` so maps can render. That traffic is handled by Apple under Apple’s terms and privacy practices, not by a FishDeck backend.

There is no FishDeck account login, no FishDeck cloud backup, no in-app purchases, no ads network, and no analytics SDK in this v1.0 build.

Sharing you initiate

If you export, share, or save a catch card or photo, that image leaves the app under your control (for example Messages, Photos, or another app you choose). We cannot recall images you already shared outside FishDeck.

Children

FishDeck is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app to store someone else’s personal information without an appropriate basis to do so.

Data deletion

Because v1.0 is local-first and has no FishDeck account:

  • Delete individual catches inside the app when that control is available
  • Remove photos from a catch inside the app when that control is available
  • Uninstall the app to remove remaining FishDeck local data from that device

There is no cloud copy for us to delete in v1.0.

Changes

If FishDeck later adds accounts, cloud sync, analytics, or other data practices, we will update this Privacy Policy and App Store privacy disclosures before those features ship.
