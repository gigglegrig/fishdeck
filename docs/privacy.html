OhMyCatch Privacy Support

Privacy Policy

Your journal stays yours.

This policy explains what OhMyCatch keeps on your device, which permissions are optional, and when outside services are used.

Applies to OhMyCatch iOS & Android (v1.0.0, v1.1.0, and future releases) · Last updated July 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how OhMyCatch handles user data across all supported mobile operating systems (including iOS and Android) and all app releases (including version 1.0.0, version 1.1.0, and future local-first releases).

Who we are

OhMyCatch is a personal fishing journal app published by XI SUN (“we”, “us”), operating as gigglegrig / OhMyCatch.

Contact for privacy questions: sx4490550@gmail.com (include “Privacy” in the subject).

Summary

OhMyCatch is a local-first application that stores your fishing catch records directly on your device. We do not require an account. We do not operate an OhMyCatch cloud backend for your catch data. We do not sell your data. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking analytics SDKs.

Information stored on your device

Depending on how you use the app, OhMyCatch may store on your device:

  • Catch records you create (species, date/time, notes, measurements, and related journal fields)
  • Photos you attach to catches (camera or photo library)
  • Location coordinates you choose to record for a catch, or that are read from a photo’s EXIF metadata when available
  • Local preferences and UI session state (for example filters, unit preferences, or last-used views)
  • Bundled catalog and artwork that ships with the app

This information stays in on-device storage (including local SQLite and app storage). Uninstalling OhMyCatch removes the app’s local data from that device, subject to normal operating system storage behavior.

Permissions you control

OhMyCatch may ask for permissions on your device:

PermissionWhy
Location (When In Use / Foreground)Show your position on the map and record catch locations when you choose
CameraTake catch photos
Photo Library / Media AccessAttach existing photos to catches or save catch cards to your photo library

You can deny any permission. Core journaling still works without location or photos; related features are limited until you allow them.

Network use

OhMyCatch does not upload your catch journal, photos, notes, or exact fishing coordinates to an OhMyCatch server.

The app may load map tiles and related location services through standard system map components (Apple Maps / Google Maps via react-native-maps) so maps can render. That traffic is handled directly by Apple or Google under their respective terms and privacy practices, not by an OhMyCatch backend.

There is no OhMyCatch account login, no OhMyCatch cloud backup, no in-app purchases, no advertising network, and no tracking analytics SDK in the app.

Sharing you initiate

If you export, share, or save a catch card or photo, that image leaves the app under your control (for example Messages, Photos, or another app you choose). We cannot recall images you already shared outside OhMyCatch.

Children

OhMyCatch is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app to store someone else’s personal information without an appropriate basis to do so.

Data deletion

Because OhMyCatch is local-first and has no user accounts:

  • Delete individual catches inside the app when that control is available
  • Remove photos from a catch inside the app when that control is available
  • Uninstall the app to remove remaining OhMyCatch local data from that device

There is no cloud copy for us to delete.

Changes

If OhMyCatch later adds accounts, cloud sync, analytics, or other data practices in a future update, we will update this Privacy Policy and App Store / Play Store privacy disclosures before those features ship.
